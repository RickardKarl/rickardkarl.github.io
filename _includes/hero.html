The PC algorithm working out a causal graph. It starts with every variable connected to every other, then tests pairs of variables for independence, one at a time. Whenever two variables are independent given some of the others, the edge between them is removed. Edges left without an arrowhead are the ones whose direction the tests alone can't tell. Spirtes & Glymour, “An algorithm for fast recovery of sparse causal graphs,” Social Science Computer Review, 1991.
