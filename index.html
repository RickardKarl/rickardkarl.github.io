The PC algorithm working out a causal graph. It starts with every variable connected to every other, and removes an edge whenever two variables are independent given some of the others. Edges left without an arrowhead are the ones whose direction can't be told from independences alone. Spirtes & Glymour, “An algorithm for fast recovery of sparse causal graphs,” Social Science Computer Review, 1991.
