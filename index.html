About Research

Hi, I'm Rickard, a scientist and engineer working as a founding member of technical staff on a yet-to-be-revealed startup.

I recently completed my PhD in Computer Science (cum laude) at Delft University of Technology. I'm originally from Sweden, where I did my BSc in Engineering Physics and MSc in Engineering Mathematics at Chalmers University of Technology. Along the way, I've also worked at Harvard, Booking.com, and NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.

Alongside my research, I've developed several open-source tools, including causal-falsify, a Python package implementing methods to detect unmeasured confounding in observational data, and rct-checker, an LLM-based tool that extracts table data from medical papers on randomized controlled trials and checks it for statistical irregularities.
